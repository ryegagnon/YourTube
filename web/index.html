Channel guide

TV remote: up and down or the channel buttons change channel, left and right set the volume (in the guide they move through time), Info shows what is on and what is next, OK opens this guide and picks a channel, Back leaves it. Red opens the guide, green settings, yellow smaller text, blue larger text. Keyboard: G guide, S settings, M mute, F full screen, plus and minus text size. Type a channel number to jump to it.

YourTube

Your YouTube channels and playlists, running like live TV. Your lineup and volume are remembered next time.

Settings

Channel 1 is always the guide. Your channels start at 2. Type a new number in a channel's first box to move it; the channel already there swaps into its old spot.

Paste any YouTube channel link (like youtube.com/@name) or playlist link, then choose Add channel. Leave the name blank to use the channel's own name.
